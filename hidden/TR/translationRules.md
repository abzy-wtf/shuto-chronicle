### The master lexicon was built from 2025 terminology and Genki-sourced translations (EN, ES, CN).

## RU:

Follows the Polivanov style of translation. 2025 files were translated from JP -> RU using the rest of the 2025 localizations as a benchmark. Master lexicon was updated to be as close to original texts as possible.

## EN:

Case-literal. No colorful or 'purple' prose. All existing EN translations use 2025 and the master lexicon as a standard.

## ES:

Follows European Castilan Spanish. All existing ES translations use 2025 and the master lexicon as a standard.

## CN:

Case-for-case Simplified. All existing CN translations use 2025 and the master lexicon as a standard.
